01

How to delete the account

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the CarHelp app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Data and account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email (without access to the app)

Send an email to support@carhelp.pt, from the account’s email address, with the subject “Delete CarHelp account”. We delete the account and reply to confirm within 30 days at most.

02

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email, ID and profile photo).
  • The cars you are the sole owner of, with their maintenance history, invoices, reminders and photos.
  • All checks (quotes, dashboard lights, sounds, OBD codes, symptoms) and the associated photos and recordings.
  • The chat with the assistant, notifications and sharing requests sent and received.
  • The files saved in your cloud storage folder.
03

What is kept, and for how long

  • Shared cars: if you shared a car with another account, that car is not deleted; it passes to the other member and your account is removed from it. That car’s data continues to belong to the new owner.
  • Support messages you sent us: kept for as long as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations.
  • Technical copies and logs in providers’ systems (for example, security logs): may remain for a limited period until they are deleted automatically.
  • Purchases: the subscription record stays in your Google Play account, managed by Google, and is not affected by this deletion. Deleting your CarHelp account does not cancel the subscription: cancel it in Play Store › Payments & subscriptions.
04

Before deleting

If you want to keep a copy, export it in Profile › Data and account (backup and PDF report). Deletion cannot be undone.
