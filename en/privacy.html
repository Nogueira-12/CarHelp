01

Who we are

The CarHelp app is provided by Nogueira Dev (independent developer, Portugal), the controller of the data described in this policy. For any privacy question: support@carhelp.pt.

02

03

Who processes the data

We use providers that process data on our behalf and according to our instructions:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage and functions): stores your account and your app data.
  • AI service: when you request a check or use the chat, the relevant text, photos and sounds, as well as some car data (make, model, year, fuel, mileage), are sent to an AI provider to generate the answer. Currently this is Google (Gemini API); the provider may change, and this policy will be updated.
  • Google AdMob: advertising in the version without a subscription. Google may process the advertising ID and technical device data, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Play: processes subscriptions and payments. We never see your card details; we only receive confirmation that the subscription is active.
  • Resend: sending the app’s emails (verification codes and support replies).

We do not sell your data or use it to profile you. Some providers may process data outside the European Union, with the applicable legal safeguards (for example, standard contractual clauses).

04

Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract: account, garage, checks, chat, subscription.
  • Consent: access to the microphone, photos and notifications (which you can withdraw in your phone settings) and, where applicable, personalised ads.
  • Legitimate interest: security, abuse prevention and responding to support requests.
05

How long we keep it

  • Account and garage data are kept for as long as the account exists.
  • When you delete the account, the data is erased (see Delete account).
  • Email verification codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • Support messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations.
06

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. In the app you can correct your data, export a copy (Profile › Data and account) and delete your account. For any other request, write to support@carhelp.pt; we reply within one month at most.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD).

07

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Google services with per-account access control. No system is completely immune to failures, but we take reasonable measures to protect it.

08

Minors

CarHelp is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

09

Changes

We may update this policy. The update date is at the top; if the change is significant, we will let you know in the app.
